Where your data lives

disqo jobs doesn't collect your data. There's no account and no server behind this app — your resume, your jobs, and your log stay on this computer, in the folder below. It sits outside the app folder, so updating or deleting disqo jobs never touches your records.

Two exceptions, neither of which sends anything about you. The Star on GitHub button in the footer is GitHub's own embed, so your browser loads it from github.com on each page — that reveals your IP to GitHub, as visiting any site would. And AI: when you use scoring or application writing, the job posting and the relevant parts of your resume are sent to your AI provider to be processed. In copy-and-paste mode nothing is sent automatically at all — you choose what to paste.

Keep a copy somewhere else

Backups on the same laptop don't survive a lost or broken laptop. Two options:

  • Drag the backups folder into Dropbox, iCloud Drive, or Google Drive every so often — or point disqo jobs at a synced folder by setting JOBPILOT_DATA_DIR.
  • Email yourself the spreadsheet once a week. It's small, and a dated email is its own timestamped record if an agency ever asks.

Sync the backups rather than the live database — cloud services can corrupt a database file that's being written while it syncs.

Restore from a backup

Replaces everything currently in disqo jobs with the contents of a backup file (jobpilot-YYYY-MM-DD.db). Your current database is saved alongside the backups first, so this is reversible.
