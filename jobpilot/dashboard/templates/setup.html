{# ---------------------------------------------------------------- #} {% elif step == 'cv' %}

Want a full CV as well?

For every job it prepares, disqo jobs writes a one-page tailored resume and a cover letter. It can also write a full-length CV — your complete history with every role, education and skills, tailored to the posting but not condensed. Useful for academic, research, and government roles, and in countries where employers expect a CV.

You can change this any time in Settings.

Use it on your phone?

The dashboard works on a phone screen. Turn this on and any device on the same Wi-Fi — your phone, a tablet, another computer — can open disqo jobs to review jobs and check your log. Everything still stays in your home: nothing goes through the internet.

Turning it on takes effect the next time you start disqo jobs.
